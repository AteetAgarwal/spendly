Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and use our services. This includes:

  • Account Information: Email address and password.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually enter.
  • Usage Data: Basic logs about how you interact with the application to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of Spendly. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Maintain your personal expense tracking dashboard.
  • Generate spending patterns and category breakdowns.
  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We use industry-standard encryption for passwords and secure connections (HTTPS) to protect your data during transmission.

You have the right to request a copy of your data or ask for your account and all associated data to be permanently deleted.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently integrate with third-party financial institutions or share data with outside services. If we introduce such features in the future, we will update this policy and notify you explicitly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
